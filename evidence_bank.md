# Evidence Bank: Abstract, Introduction, and Method

## Scope and precedence

This bank supports the current partial manuscript only: the Abstract,
Introduction, and Method of **Flow-KTO: Learning Flow-Based Robot Policies from
Unary Feedback**. Experiment and validation prose is deliberately out of scope.
When a historical project log conflicts with an explicit user confirmation,
the user confirmation controls the manuscript. In particular, the manuscript
objective is exact KTO, `lambda_y [1 - sigma(beta m_y)]`; historical softplus
runs remain development evidence and do not define the method equation.

External papers support definitions and positioning. They do not validate
Flow-KTO's empirical claims.

## Current evidence decision

The three sections may define and derive Flow-KTO, state the restricted-feedback
problem, and distinguish prior assumptions. All effect claims remain blank in
the current draft. The operational rate table, statistical claims, and any
statement connecting the policy-centered reference or beta treatment to
deployment improvement require the deferred Experiments and Validation
sections.
